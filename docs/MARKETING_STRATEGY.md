# Marketing & Growth Strategy - AI Automation Agency

## Phase 4: Multi-Channel Marketing Framework

### Target Customer Profiles

#### Primary ICP (Ideal Customer Profile)
**Mid-Market Healthcare Practices**
- **Size**: 5-50 employees, $2M-$20M revenue
- **Pain Points**: Staff shortage, administrative burden, patient satisfaction
- **Budget**: $5K-$50K annual technology spend
- **Decision Makers**: Practice Manager, Medical Director, IT Director

**Professional Services Firms**
- **Size**: 10-100 employees, $5M-$50M revenue
- **Types**: Legal firms, accounting practices, consulting companies
- **Pain Points**: Client communication, appointment scheduling, document processing
- **Budget**: $10K-$100K annual automation spend

**E-commerce & Retail**
- **Size**: $1M-$100M annual revenue
- **Pain Points**: Customer support volume, order processing, inventory management
- **Budget**: $15K-$200K annual customer service spend
- **Decision Makers**: Operations Director, Customer Experience Manager

### Lead Generation Strategy

#### Inbound Marketing
**Website Optimization**
- Landing pages for each industry vertical
- Interactive ROI calculators
- Free resources (whitepapers, templates, guides)
- Live chat with qualification bot
- Demo scheduling integration

**SEO Strategy**
- **Primary Keywords**: "AI phone agents", "healthcare automation", "customer service AI"
- **Long-tail Keywords**: "HIPAA compliant AI chatbot", "legal practice automation software"
- **Local SEO**: Target major metropolitan areas
- **Technical SEO**: Page speed, mobile optimization, schema markup

#### Outbound Marketing
**Cold Email Campaigns**
- **Healthcare Sequence**: 7-email sequence targeting practice managers
- **Legal Sequence**: 5-email sequence for law firm administrators
- **E-commerce Sequence**: 6-email sequence for customer service directors

**LinkedIn Outreach**
- **Daily Target**: 50 connection requests
- **Follow-up Sequence**: 4-message sequence over 2 weeks
- **Content Strategy**: Share industry insights and case studies

**Cold Calling Program**
- **Daily Target**: 100 calls per SDR
